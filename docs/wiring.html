Power rules

  • Set the boost converter to 5.0 V with a multimeter first. The MT3608 is adjustable, and many arrive set to 9–12 V, which would destroy the ESP32.
  • Battery never goes straight to VIN or 3V3. On VIN, the board's regulator browns out below about 4.3 V. On 3V3, a full cell (4.2 V) is over the ESP32's limit.
  • Charge with the switch OFF. A TP4056 can't run the load and charge correctly at the same time.
  • Don't plug in the ESP32's USB while the switch is ON. Two 5 V sources would feed each other. Switch OFF to flash or debug.
  • Use a protected cell or a TP4056 board with the protection chip (6 pins: IN± B± OUT±). The 4-pin version has no over-discharge cutoff.

First power-on

  1. Leave the battery unplugged. With a multimeter, check there is no short between 3V3 and GND, or between VIN and GND.
  2. Power from USB only. The serial monitor (115200 baud) should show WHO_AM_I=0x70, and the OLED should show CALIBRATING and then MONITORING.
  3. Tap CANCEL, hold SOS for 1 s: the buzzer and red LED should start; CANCEL silences them.
  4. Only then: set the boost to 5.0 V with no load, wire it to VIN, unplug USB, switch ON.

The motion sensor and OLED wiring is verified on hardware. The buzzer, buttons, LEDs and battery chain follow the firmware pin map but have not been tested on the bench yet.
